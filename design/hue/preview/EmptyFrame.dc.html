The empty-state frame

One shared frame for every page that can be empty: the words on the left, a short scene of the page at work on the right. Each page brings its own words and its own scene; the frame never changes.

The rules

Headingwhat the page is for“Every student, with their family”, never “Nothing here yet”.
Textone or two sentencesWhy it is empty, and what appears here once it is used.
Buttonone, that fixes itImport, add, set up, ask Gradeon. A second link only when it is truly needed. Never a dead end.
Sceneone story, then loopUp to six steps over 10 seconds, built from the page's own parts: rows, tags, cards, bars. No pictures or icons.
Examplessaid soNames and numbers are made up and a caption says they are examples.
Decorativefor the eye onlyThe scene is hidden from screen readers and nothing in it can be clicked.
Colourtokens onlyLight, dark and every school colour work without changes. See the dark boards.
Narrowbelow 640 pxThe halves stack, the scene on top, the button full width at the bottom.
Less motionwhen the phone asksWith reduced motion on, the scene shows its last frame and stays still.
Whenonly when truly emptyLoading and error states stay as they are. A filter with no results is not an empty page.
Playingthe scene runs its story, then starts again

The 10-second story

every scene uses the same six beats
10.6 s21.8 s33.0 s44.2 s55.4 s66.6 sHoldto 9.2 s, then fade and loop
Each part enters on its beat by rising, growing, popping or wiping in. A part that is replaced fades at the beat its successor arrives. Numbers count up to their final value.
