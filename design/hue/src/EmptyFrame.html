The empty-state kit

There is no shared layout. Every empty page is composed from what that page does. What they share is the kit: the words, the scene parts, the motion beats and the rules below.

The rules

Compositionits own, every pageDraw the page's own thing: the timetable grid, a receipt, a shelf, a route. The words go where that thing leaves room.
Headingwhat the page is for“Every student, with their family”, never “Nothing here yet”.
Textone or two sentencesWhy it is empty, and what appears once it is used.
Buttonone, that fixes itA second link only when it is truly needed. Never a dead end.
Sceneone story, then loopUp to six beats in 10 seconds, built from the product's own parts. No pictures or icons inside it.
Examplessaid so, neutralMade-up, international names; amounts without a currency; a caption says they are examples.
Decorativefor the eye onlyHidden from screen readers; nothing in it can be clicked.
Colourtokens onlyWorks in light, dark and every school colour without changes.
Narrowbelow 640 pxEvery composition falls back to one column; on the phone each page has its own arrangement.
Less motionwhen askedThe last frame, still.
Whenonly when truly emptyLoading and error states stay as they are. A filter with no results is not an empty page.
